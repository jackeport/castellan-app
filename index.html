Castellan

Castellan is a personal assistant that runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It keeps a private, local record of the owner's own activity (calendar, tasks, files, school deadlines) so it can answer questions like "what was I working on?" and "what is due this week?".

It is a private, single-user tool. It is not offered to the public, has no accounts and no servers, and only its owner signs in to it.
